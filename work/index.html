// Case study

HVAC dispatch and revenue engine

A full-lifecycle GoHighLevel build for residential and commercial HVAC contractors, aimed at the 3 big profit leaks: missed calls, slow emergency dispatch and unclosed quotes.

Watch the full walkthrough

Sequence 01

Speed-to-lead: missed call text back

Fires on any missed call, waits 1 minute, creates the opportunity, sends a reassuring triage text and alerts dispatch.

Sequence 02

Emergency service and quote intake

A mobile-first intake form that captures details, address and urgency so no-heat or no-AC outages jump the queue.

Sequence 03

Inbound form triage and routing

If emergency: instant reassurance SMS and on-call tech alert. If routine: a self-serve booking link.

Sequence 04

Service and estimate dispatch

Diagnostic and estimate scheduling with buffers, round-robin technician dispatch, reminders and rescheduling links.

Sequence 05

Nurture: unsold estimate follow-up

A 2-hour check-in SMS, a 2-day energy savings and rebates email, and a 5-day decision SMS that close quotes without manual chasing.

Sequence 06

Reputation: post-service review request

When a job is marked won, waits 1 hour and sends a direct 5-star Google review link while satisfaction is highest.
